HOW TO PLAY

Goal

Each mission has a primary objective (red pointer) and optional jobs. Drive the truck into an EXIT zone, or walk out through the EVAC gate. Finishing the primary unlocks the next mission. Optional rescues and equipment help at the garage but are never required.

Noise

The dead follow sound, not magic. Footsteps, gunfire and especially the truck engine pull them in. The crowbar is quiet. A noise maker or the car alarm drags them away.

Cargo

Backpack and truck bed hold 6 units each. Small items are 1 unit. Heavy equipment (generator, refrigeration unit, filtration unit, power pack) is 3 units, must be hauled by hand and only rides in the truck bed. Park close and plan the route before you lift. On foot you keep only your backpack.

Machines and water

Switches, compressors, pumps and speakers make noise that pulls the dead. Watch for the warm-up warning, then use it or get clear. Shallow water slows everyone and every step splashes. Flooded channels stop the truck but never people.
